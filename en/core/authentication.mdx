## Sign In with Your Preferred Provider

Teamova supports single sign-on (SSO) so you can access your account quickly and securely using an identity you already trust.

  <Card title="Microsoft" icon="microsoft">

  </Card>
  <Card title="GitHub" icon="github">
    Connect with your GitHub identity.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Simplified Sign-In

<Tip>
  Teamova remembers your last sign-in method. When you return to the login page, your usual provider button is automatically highlighted so you can sign in faster.
</Tip>

No more guessing which account you used last time. Teamova detects your preferred provider and puts it front and center every time you log in.

## Link Multiple Accounts

You can connect Google, Microsoft, and GitHub to the same Teamova account. This gives you the flexibility to sign in with whichever provider is most convenient at the moment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Account Settings">
    Open the user menu and navigate to your account settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Linked Accounts">
    In the security section, locate the linked accounts panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a Provider">
    Click the provider you want to link and authorize the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch Anytime">
    On your next sign-in, choose any of your linked providers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set a Backup Password

Even if you signed up with Google, Microsoft, or GitHub, you can add a password as an alternative sign-in method. This is useful if your SSO provider is temporarily unavailable or if you prefer typing credentials on certain devices.

<Note>
  Setting a backup password does not disable your SSO providers. You can continue using both methods interchangeably.
</Note>
