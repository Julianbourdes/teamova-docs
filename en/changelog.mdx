<Update label="0.6.0" tags={["Minor", "New features"]}>

## MCP Server — Connect Teamova to AI Assistants

You can now connect your Teamova workspace to Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, or Gemini using the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Ask your AI assistant about your Planning Poker sessions in natural language.

- **View sessions** — List, filter, and get detailed results from your Planning Poker sessions directly in your AI assistant
- **Create sessions** — Set up new Planning Poker sessions with guided prompts for deck type and team selection
- **Add stories** — Batch-add stories to existing sessions without leaving your AI conversation
- **Secure by design** — OAuth 2.1 authentication with fine-grained permissions ensures your data stays protected

</Update>


<Update label="0.5.0" tags={["Minor","New features","Improvements"]}>
  ## Fixes & Improvements

  Comprehensive quality assurance pass addressing 22\+ issues reported during manual testing.

  - **Vote results stability** - Vote results and statistics now persist when switching between estimated stories, fixing a flickering issue caused by a WebSocket race condition
  - **Vote results visibility** - Vote results panel is now properly hidden during active voting rounds until votes are revealed
  - **Terms of Service modal** - No longer appears on legal pages if you haven't accepted the terms yet and you want to consult these pages
  - **Story creation** - Form fields are properly reset when creating a new story
  - **Team activity tracking** - All team operations (create, update, delete, member management) are now logged in the organization activity timeline
  - **Activity translations** - All activity action labels now display correctly in both languages
  - **Consensus calculation** - Improved formula using coefficient of variation for more accurate consensus scores

  ## New Features

  - **Unarchive sessions** - Previously archived sessions can now be restored
  - **Duplicate sessions** - Create a copy of any session with its stories
  - **Enhanced host controls** - Improved action panel for session hosts
</Update>

<Update label="February 2026" tags={["New features","Improvements"]}>
  ## Sign In Your Way

  You can now sign in to Teamova using your preferred account:

  - **Google Account** - Sign in with your Google workspace
  - **Microsoft Account** - Use your Microsoft 365 credentials
  - **GitHub Account** - Connect with your GitHub identity

  ### Connect Multiple Accounts

  Link all your accounts in one place. Switch between Google, Microsoft, and GitHub sign-in methods seamlessly from your account settings.

  ### Set a Backup Password

  For added flexibility, you can now set a password even if you signed up with Google, Microsoft, or GitHub. Perfect for when you need an alternative sign-in method.

  ## Improved Forms

  We've made forms clearer and easier to use:

  - Better visual indicators show which fields are required
  - Cleaner form layouts across organization settings, team management, and Planning Poker
  - Improved error messages to help you complete forms faster

  ## Planning Poker Enhancements

  ### Instant Notifications

  Stay in sync with your team:

  - See real-time notifications when roles change during a session
  - Get instant feedback when invitations are sent or revoked

  ### Smoother Collaboration

  We've improved the real-time experience so everyone stays on the same page during estimation sessions.

  ### Invite a Whole Team in One Click

  No more inviting participants one by one. Select a team and all its members automatically join your Planning Poker session.

  ### More Reliable Jira Import

  Fixed an issue where Jira identifiers appeared twice in the story list. The display is now clean and consistent.

  ## Teams

  Organize your collaborators into teams within your organization:

  - **Create teams** with a name, description, and distinctive color
  - **Add members** by selecting from your organization members
  - **Easily edit** your team composition at any time
  - **Invite an entire team** directly into a Planning Poker session

  Teams make daily collaboration easier and speed up the preparation of your estimation sessions.

  ## Account Management

  ### Account Deletion

  You have full control over your data. Request account deletion from your settings:

  - **90-day grace period**: change your mind at any time and reactivate your account
  - **Clear notification**: a banner shows the remaining time before permanent deletion
  - **Data export**: download all your personal data before deletion

  ### Member Management

  Administrators have new tools to manage their organization members:

  - **Member archiving**: deactivate a member without permanently deleting them
  - **Reactivation**: reinstate an archived member in one click
  - **History**: view the list of inactive members and their deactivation dates
  - **Scheduled deletion**: removed members are deleted after 90 days, with the option to cancel

  ### Organization Deletion

  Administrators can schedule the deletion of their organization:

  - **30-day grace period** before permanent deletion
  - **Email notification** sent to all organization members
  - **Cancellation possible** at any time during the grace period
  - **Full export** of all organization data

  ## Simplified Sign-In

  Teamova now remembers your last sign-in method. When you return to the login page, your usual provider button (Google, Microsoft, or GitHub) is highlighted so you can sign in faster.

  ## Improved Reliability

  We've strengthened platform stability:

  - Reduced loading times, especially on first access of the day
  - Fewer interruptions during Planning Poker sessions
  - More robust connection with your tools (Jira, sign-in providers)

  ## Plans and Features

  Teamova now offers three plans tailored to your needs:

  - **FREE**: discover Teamova with essential features
  - **PRO**: for teams that want to go further (export, advanced integrations)
  - **MAX**: for organizations with extended needs

  Some features like CSV export and Jira integration are now available based on your plan.
</Update>

<Update label="January 2026" tags={["New features"]}>
  ## Welcome to Teamova

  Teamova is now live! Here's what you can do:

  ### Planning Poker

  Run agile estimation sessions with your team in real-time:

  **Create Sessions**

  - Start a session with a unique code that anyone can join
  - Choose from Fibonacci, T-Shirt sizes, Powers of 2, or create custom decks
  - Configure voting rules (anonymous voting, auto-reveal, show cards)

  **Collaborate in Real-Time**

  - Vote simultaneously with your team
  - See results instantly when votes are revealed
  - Track consensus with visual distribution charts
  - Set final estimates with smart suggestions

  **Manage Stories**

  - Add stories manually or import from Jira
  - Include descriptions, priorities, and tags
  - Mark baseline stories for reference
  - Export results to CSV

  **Team Management**

  - Assign roles: Host, Participant, or Spectator
  - Change roles during the session
  - See who's voted and who's still deciding

  ### Jira Integration

  Connect your Jira workspace:

  - Import stories directly into your Planning Poker sessions
  - Sync estimation results back to Jira automatically
  - Filter by issue type, status, and assignee

  ### Custom Estimation Decks

  Create decks that match your team's workflow:

  - Build custom decks with your own values
  - Use pre-built templates
  - Share decks across sessions
  - Duplicate and edit existing decks

  ### Additional Features

  **Voting Timers** - Set time limits for voting rounds

  **Anonymous Voting** - Hide votes until everyone has voted

  **Spectator Mode** - Let stakeholders observe without voting

  **Session Archive** - Keep history of past estimation sessions

  **Dark Mode** - Work comfortably with automatic dark theme

  **Bilingual** - Full support for English and French
</Update>
