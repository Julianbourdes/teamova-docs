## Meeting Templates

Templates give your 1-on-1s a consistent structure. Instead of starting from scratch every time, define the sections you want and reuse them across all your meetings.

## Create a Template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the template library">
    Navigate to **One-on-One > Templates** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Template">
    Give your template a name and optional description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure sections">
    Enable or disable sections, and drag them to set the order that works best for your meetings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Your template is ready to use. Assign it to any 1-on-1 series.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Default Template

Every organization starts with a default template that includes all six sections. You can customize it or create new templates alongside it.

<Tip>
  When you create a new 1-on-1 series, the default template is automatically assigned. You can change it at any time from the series detail page.
</Tip>

## Template Library

Browse and manage all your templates in one place.

- **Duplicate** an existing template to create a variant
- **Edit** any template to adjust its sections
- **Delete** templates you no longer need — series using them will switch to the default

<Note>
  The default template cannot be deleted. It serves as a fallback when other templates are removed.
</Note>
