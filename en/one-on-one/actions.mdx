## Never Lose Track of Follow-Ups

Action items help you capture tasks that come up during your meetings and make sure they get done.

## Create an Action

During or after a meeting, add action items from the right sidebar of your session.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the action">
    Type a short description of what needs to be done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign it">
    Choose who is responsible — either the manager or the team member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a due date (optional)">
    Pick a date from the calendar if the action has a deadline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Track Progress

Each action has a status you can update as work progresses.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Pending" icon="clock">
    The action has been created but work hasn't started yet.
  </Card>
  <Card title="In Progress" icon="spinner">
    Work is underway on this action.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Done" icon="check">
    The action is complete. It appears with a strikethrough in the list.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Both participants can update the status of any action — it's a shared responsibility.

## Automatic Carry-Over

<Info>
  When you finish a meeting, any actions that are still **pending** or **in progress** are automatically moved to your next scheduled session. A **Reported** badge appears so you can see which items were carried over.
</Info>

This means you never have to manually copy unfinished tasks — they follow you from one meeting to the next until they're done.

## Session Summary

After completing a session, the summary page shows all actions in read-only mode. You can review what was discussed and what needs follow-up before your next meeting.

<Tip>
  Use the summary page as a quick reference before your next 1-on-1 to check which actions were carried over.
</Tip>
