## Schedule Your 1-on-1s

Set up regular check-ins with your team members in a few clicks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a team member">
    Select the person you want to meet with from your organization members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a frequency">
    Choose how often you meet: weekly, biweekly, monthly, or schedule a one-time session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the day and time">
    Pick the day of the week and time slot that works best for both of you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a template">
    Select a meeting template to give your sessions a consistent structure, or start with the default.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Upcoming sessions are generated automatically based on your schedule. When you complete a meeting, the next ones are topped up so you always have sessions ready.
</Tip>

## Run a Session

When it's time for your 1-on-1, start the session to open the meeting workspace.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Agenda" icon="list-check">
    Your discussion topics appear in the left sidebar. Add new topics, reorder them by drag-and-drop, and check them off as you go.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Shared Notes" icon="pen-to-square">
    The center panel is your collaborative notepad. Both participants can see the notes, and the manager can edit them in real time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Private Notes" icon="lock">
    The right panel holds your personal notes. Only you can see them — perfect for things you want to remember but not share.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Prepare an Agenda

You don't have to wait until the meeting starts. Add topics anytime before the session.

- Type a topic and press **Enter** to add it
- Drag topics to reorder them by priority
- Both the manager and team member can add topics

<Note>
  Topics added by each participant show a badge indicating who added them, so you can see at a glance whose items are on the agenda.
</Note>

## Meeting Lifecycle

Each session follows a simple flow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scheduled">
    The session is created and waiting. You can prepare topics and review your template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="In Progress">
    Click **Start** to begin the meeting. The timer starts, and you can take notes and manage actions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Completed">
    Click **Finish** to end the session. You are redirected to the summary page where you can review everything discussed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also **skip** or **cancel** a session if the meeting doesn't happen.

## Series Management

Manage your recurring 1-on-1 series over time.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Pause a series">
    Taking a break? Pause the series to stop generating new sessions. Resume whenever you're ready.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Archive a series">
    When a series is no longer needed, archive it. Past sessions remain accessible for reference.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
